Create a high-fidelity responsive UI/UX design for a next-generation University Web Portal tailored for higher education in Southeast Asia (bilingual: English and Khmer). The interface must feel modern, highly trustworthy, welcoming, and deeply human-centered, moving away from cluttered, traditional administrative layouts.

### 1. VISUAL SYSTEM & STYLE GUIDE
- Color Palette: Trustworthy Deep Slate Blue (#0F172A), Academic Gold/Amber (#F59E0B), and Crisp Clean White (#FFFFFF). Use dark slate for structure and text, gold for high-importance call-to-actions, and ample whitespace to prevent cognitive overload.
- Typography: use using this `public/fonts folder ` 
- Aesthetic: Modern, professional, and content-first. Use soft border radiuses (8px-12px), subtle drop shadows, and card-based containers (Bento Grid style) for structured dashboard elements. Avoid flashy animations that hinder performance.

### 2. CORE TARGET AUDIENCES & PERSONALIZED PORTALS
Design a global header navigation that allows quick wayfinding and audience-based routing:
- Prospective Students: Focus on "Find My Major", Campus Life, and Admissions.
- Parents: Focus on Tuitions, Financial Aid, and Student Support/Safety.
- Current Students: Access to class portals, schedules, and the Student Senate.
- Alumni: Networking, giving back, and career networks.

### 3. KEY INTERACTIVE COMPONENTS TO DESIGN
Please generate the following key views and components:

A. HERO SECTION & DYNAMIC SEARCH
- A bold, emotive hero section with high-quality photography of a diverse, modern campus.
- A prominent, centralized Smart Search Bar with an autocomplete dropdown featuring quick categories (e.g., "Scholarships", "Academic Calendar", "Housing").
- A bilingual language switcher (EN / KH) clearly visible in the top-right of the header.

B. "FIND MY MAJOR" INTERACTIVE ALGORITHM TOOL (BENTO GRID CARD)
An interactive widget card that helps high school graduates match their aspirations to degrees. It features:
- A progress indicator for a "20-minute quick survey" assessing academic strengths, interests, and skills.
- A dynamic preview of matching career paths alongside estimated job availability and potential salaries in the current market.
- Quick action button: "Explore matching degrees".

C. THE 7-STAGE STUDENT JOURNEY WAYFINDER (TIMELINE NAVIGATION)
An intuitive visual timeline representing the full student lifecycle:
- Stage 1: Awareness & Discovery (Virtual Campus Tour, Student Stories)
- Stage 2: Evaluation & Program Selection (Compare Majors side-by-side)
- Stage 3: Admissions & Financial Decision (Clear Tuition & Loan calculator)
- Stage 4: Onboarding & Transition (First-week checklist & Orientation hub)
- Stage 5: Academic Engagement (Faculty directory & course registration)
- Stage 6: Graduation & Career Launch (Internship links & Job board)
- Stage 7: Alumni Connection (Giving & mentoring opportunities)

### 4. ACCESSIBILITY & USABILITY STANDARDS (WCAG & ADA Compliant)
- Ensure high contrast ratios (minimum 4.5:1) for all text elements.
- Clean mobile-first layouts featuring thumb-friendly touch targets (min 44x44px for buttons).
- Plain, human language; avoid administrative or academic jargon.
- Clear wayfinding cues (e.g., visible breadcrumbs, descriptive page titles, and sticky navigation headers).
